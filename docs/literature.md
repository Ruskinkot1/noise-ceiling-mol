## Verdict: partly taken

- Taken: (a) ChEMBL inter-assay noise (1.1, 1.2); (b) the idea of a "performance bound due to measurement error" (K2) and an inter-laboratory ceiling in the special case of PROTAC (K1); (c) "embeddings do not beat ECFP" (K3, 3.8 from an excerpt); (d) audits of leakage and label conflicts in TDC/MoleculeNet (1.4, 5.2); (e) guidance on method comparison and statistical tests (6.1, 6.2).
- Free (according to this search): the combination of (1) a ceiling from real replicates across different ChEMBL documents on 15+ targets/endpoints, (2) fraction of ceiling as a metric for comparing foundation models and ECFP+GBM under random/scaffold/temporal splits, (3) MDE and a protocol tied to noise.
- New compared to NOVELTY.md: K1 (2026) confirms that an "inter-laboratory ceiling" is already being published, so priority cannot be claimed on the term and idea itself; 6.1 overlaps with the "reporting protocol" more strongly than expected.
- Proposed narrowing: frame the contribution as "**A comparison of pretrained embeddings and ECFP+GBM in units of fraction of the noise ceiling on replicates from independent ChEMBL documents, with MDE**". Do not claim "the first ceiling" or "the first embedding benchmark". Cite K1, K2, K3, 1.1, 6.1 and contrast: empirical replicates vs assumed error (K2); many endpoints and ECFP vs a single PROTAC set (K1); absolute metrics vs fraction of ceiling (K3, 6.1).
- Search limitations: full texts were not read; the RSC page is unavailable; WebSearch returns excerpts, not all numbers were verified; the 2026 preprints are not peer-reviewed; closed pharma reports and works not indexed by search are not covered.
