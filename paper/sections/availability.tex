\section{Data and code availability}
Code, configuration, the extracted ChEMBL data and all results of this paper are available at \url{https://github.com/Ruskinkot1/noise-ceiling-mol/tree/full-experiment-preprint} (tag \texttt{preprint-v1}); the \texttt{README} describes how to regenerate every table and figure from the stored results in minutes, or to rerun the models in about one and a half days on a laptop. The repository contains the per-task molecule and measurement files (identifier, SMILES, label, number of documents, year of first report), the per-document measurements, and the predictions of every model, split and seed. The pretrained-model embeddings (about 400\,MB) and the registry of public benchmark molecules used for decontamination are not stored but are regenerated by \texttt{scripts/06\_embed\_foundation.py} and \texttt{scripts/build\_registry.py}. Because \texttt{scripts/01\_extract\_chembl.py} queries the live ChEMBL API, rerunning the extraction will give a later release than the ChEMBL~37 snapshot used here.
